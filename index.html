Privacy Policy — Production House Tycoon

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Android app com.nivro.productionsim, published by Nivro.

The game runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit or store your personal data anywhere, it has no account or sign-in, it contains no advertising, and it does no analytics or tracking of any kind.

The only time the app talks to the internet at all is if you choose to buy the optional Executive Producer supporter tier, which is handled by Google Play.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app saves is written to its own private storage on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your saved games — your studio, staff, projects, finances and history.
  • Your settings, including whether you have dismissed the opening guide.
  • Cover images you choose. If you pick a picture from your gallery to use as a show's cover, the app copies that one file into its own folder so it does not disappear when your gallery changes. The app has no access to the rest of your gallery, only to the single image you selected. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • A record of your purchase, if you buy Executive Producer — specifically the Google Play order ID, so the app can show it to you again later.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, and no way for us to recover it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any device identifier.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • It does not send your saved games or any other content to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

Purchases

The game is a paid app, and it also offers one optional in-app purchase: the Executive Producer supporter tier.

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your payment details — no card number, no billing address, nothing. Google tells the app only that a purchase completed, along with the order ID for it. Google Play's own handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

The app requests the com.android.vending.BILLING and INTERNET permissions solely so that Google Play Billing can work. No other part of the game uses the network.

The Executive Producer group

If you buy Executive Producer, you may choose to join a private group chat run by the developer. This is entirely optional — the purchase does not require it, and nothing in the game depends on it.

If you choose to join:

  • You send us your Google Play order ID so we can confirm the purchase and approve your request. We keep it only to check it against Play's order records and to know who is in the group.
  • The group is hosted on a third-party messaging platform, which has its own terms and privacy policy. What that platform collects, and what it shows to other members of the group, is governed by its policy and not by this one. We do not control it. The app names the platform and links to it at the point where you choose to join, so you can read its policy before you do.
  • You can leave the group at any time.

We do not add you to any mailing list and we have no way to email you.

Children

The game is not directed at children. It is a business simulation with no objectionable content, but it is written for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — of any age — because the app collects no personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date at the top will change. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes unless the app itself gains a feature that needs them.
